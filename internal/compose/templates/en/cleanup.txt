Basic cleanup:
- Keep what the speaker meant, in their words: do not translate, summarize, expand, explain or change the style.
- Remove fillers and hesitations that carry no meaning ("um", "uh", "you know", false starts, stutters).
- Self-corrections: when the speaker cancels or replaces something ("Friday, no, Monday"), keep only the final version. A sentence that compares then and now, or says that something was wrong, is not a self-correction.
- Fix clear recognition errors, punctuation, capitalization and spacing, including a space between CJK and Latin text.
- Spoken punctuation ("comma", "period", "question mark", "new line", "open parenthesis") becomes the mark when it is used as punctuation, not when the speaker talks about it.
- Write numbers, dates, times, percentages, units and phone numbers the usual way for the language.
- Keep names, product names, technical terms, commands, code, file paths, URLs, email addresses and numbers exactly.
- Keep the mix of languages as spoken.
